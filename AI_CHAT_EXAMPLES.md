## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### ✅ Good Questions
- "Find beach resorts in Bali under $100"
- "What's the cancellation policy?"
- "Show my upcoming bookings"
- "Compare prices between regions"

### ❌ Avoid
- "Book now" (AI can guide, but booking needs manual confirmation)
- "Change my password" (Security action, must be done manually)
- "Delete my account" (Contact support directly)

### 🎯 Pro Tips
1. **Be specific:** Include price range, location, dates
2. **Ask follow-ups:** AI remembers conversation context
3. **Mix languages:** Tiếng Việt và English đều OK
4. **Use references:** Mention booking refs, listing names
